      Platinum difficulty

      5.5 / 10

      No difficulty-setting trophies and no mission gold medals: the ceiling is The Shootist's 42 range golds and Gunslinger's five-in-one Focus. Everything else is volume, not virtuosity.

      Time to platinum

      75–95 hours

      Roughly 35–45 for the story and its arcs, 10–15 sweeping collectibles, and 25–30 on mastery and money. Following this roadmap's order is what keeps it at the low end.
